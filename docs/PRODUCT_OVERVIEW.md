# Product Overview

**YouSee** is a human-centered urban intelligence platform. **SheLens** is its first thematic initiative, focused on women's urban experiences. Pilot city: Bandar Lampung.

## Five experiences
1. **Explore** (`/explore`): map with emotion and district layers, filters, district context, official source links.
2. **Share** (`/share`): 3-step mobile-first form covering issue, experience or cognitive map; feeling; observation; photo; point, route or area; precision; consent.
3. **Understand** (`/understand`): topic and feeling charts, coverage gaps, representation notes, insights with full traceability and community validation.
4. **Connect** (`/connect`): Ask Your City (grounded, cited) and verified resources.
5. **Create Possibilities** (`/possibilities`): proposals with an evidence-gated status ladder and support.

## Roles in the MVP
Citizens (no account) · Reviewers (validation form, self-declared role) · Moderators (passcode admin). Researcher and institutional views are planned.

## Platform extensibility
`initiatives` and `cities` tables let new thematic initiatives and cities reuse the same pipeline without code changes to the core.
